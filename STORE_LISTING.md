# Store listing

Name: **Pebble Like Time**  
Category: Daily  
Platform: Pebble Time 2 (Emery)

## Short description

A crisp clock with battery, date, Health stats and locally calculated sun times.

## Description

Pebble Like Time combines a large clock with Workout-yellow status panels and
fine dotted separators. It follows your watch's 12/24-hour and date settings.

See battery percentage and its visual fill level, daily steps, heart rate,
sunrise and sunset at a glance.
Unavailable status rows disappear and the clock recenters automatically.
The clock updates once per minute. No settings, accounts, ads or analytics.

Health data stays on the watch. Sun times are calculated locally with SunCalc
1.9.0, normally once per day, without web requests. Coordinates are not stored
by the app. The phone's location provider may use GPS, Wi-Fi or cellular services.
Sun times require a paired phone and location permission; they are approximate
and may retain an earlier location until the next day. Polar day/night hides
sun times. The clock works without location access.

Independent watchface; not an official Pebble or Core Devices app.
Developed with the assistance of coding AI.
